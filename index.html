Do I need the Studio plugin?

              
Chat and build entirely in the browser if you want. The free plugin is what lets ROTEX apply edits to your open place live in Roblox Studio.

            

            

              
Is Pro a subscription?

              
No. Pro is a one-time $20 purchase that lasts 30 days. It never auto-renews — when it ends, you just drop back to the free plan.

            

            

              
What can ROTEX actually build?

              
Whole systems: shops, pet systems, obbies, round-based games, daily rewards, leaderstats, UI, DataStores, remotes — complete scripts, not fragments.

            

            

              
Is the code safe for real games?

              
ROTEX writes server-authoritative code by default — purchases and rewards are validated on the server, so exploiters can't fake them from the client.
